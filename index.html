Our Story

Welcome to Restaurant Filarakia

Nestled on the beautiful shores of Kos, Filarakia brings together the warmth of Greek hospitality and the comfort of Dutch cuisine. Whether you're seeking a refreshing cocktail at sunset, fresh seafood, or hearty Dutch specialties, our restaurant awaits you.

🍽️

Greek & Dutch Fusion

Experience the best of both worlds with our unique blend of Mediterranean and Dutch culinary traditions.

🌅

Beachfront Paradise

Dine with breathtaking views of the Aegean Sea and enjoy magical sunsets every evening.

🤝

Warm Hospitality

Filarakia means "friends" in Greek — and that's exactly how we treat every guest who walks through our door.
